# How we worked, and what we would change

Updated 18 October 2026. This account separates what happened from the approach we would try next. It is a record of one project, not a comparison of workflows under controlled conditions.

## How the work developed

The book began with a question, then acquired a chapter plan and an argument. AI contributed substantially to research, drafting, review, and production. The author's questions and reactions repeatedly changed the direction.

Once a complete draft existed, we asked separately whether its argument held together, its sources supported its claims, and its prose was enjoyable. Comparing passages with accessible nonfiction helped us see that our draft often summarized a discovery before letting the reader encounter it. We tried different treatments, particularly in the sleep chapter, and revised the larger manuscript.

Later reviews without the custom prose skill, and reviews and rewrites from other models, found problems the earlier passes had not resolved. Several chapters sounded too similar even when their subjects differed. Repeated qualifications slowed the reading. Some illustrations worked much better when an attempted solution revealed a new problem. The garden became important because greater understanding could leave a conflict intact.

We compared the external rewrites with the saved manuscript in chunks, then considered the whole book. That helped distinguish local improvements from changes that repeated or weakened material elsewhere. We retained the saved manuscript as the base. The final integration edit restored a few useful sentences and some warmth, clarified two claims, and kept the stronger individual-difference example and additional source work.

## Instructions helped carry checks, not guarantee results

The editing skill preserved intentions between tasks: retain uncertainty where it matters, distinguish an illustration from evidence, inspect support for added detail, and compare an edit with its original. It was useful to have these expectations written down.

The weakness was treating compliance as evidence that a reader would enjoy the result. A passage could obey the instructions and still be predictable. Removing a familiar sentence pattern could also remove a helpful distinction. Our later comparison recovered the stress chapter's line, “The work has become clearer, not more possible,” for that reason.

The final integration edit did not apply the self-created prose skill. Its archived instructions remain available as part of the project's history, with their license and attribution intact. We cannot separate the skill's effects from the prompts, models, drafts, and editorial decisions that changed alongside it.

## Why separate the jobs

The most persistent difficulty was the relationship between scientific caution and reading rhythm. A finding, a limitation, a counterweight, and an ethical conclusion became a reliable sequence. Keeping that sequence could feel responsible locally while flattening a whole chapter. Simply deleting cautious words would have risked changing the claims without changing the underlying structure.

We would use a narrower skill again, with a few requirements active throughout: do not invent observations, do not strengthen a claim beyond its support, and keep the distinctions the reader needs to interpret it. We would not make every drafting instruction carry the full prose, scientific, structural, and production review at once.

For a new chapter, the practical sequence would be:

1. Develop the explanation around its actual question and evidence. Let its shape follow what the reader needs to discover.
2. Read for interest and understanding without the custom prose skill or a defense of the draft. Identify exact passages that drag, repeat, or confuse.
3. Revise those passages, then check the changed claims and the qualifications they still need. Put methodological detail in notes only when the main text remains accurate on its own.
4. Read adjoining chapters together. A good paragraph can still repeat a move the reader has encountered too often.

That is our proposed next approach, not an experimentally established advantage of multiple passes. Some course correction is unavoidable. If the same correction keeps recurring, change the instruction that encourages the problem rather than adding another permanent cleanup pass. Actual reader response remains necessary to judge whether the work is enjoyable.

## Continuing work and reading afresh

A continuing conversation helped preserve decisions: why a chapter existed, why a caveat mattered, and why an attractive rewrite had been rejected. But a reviewer who has followed all that discussion can read the intended book into the actual words.

A fresh conversation supplied a useful change of conditions. We could provide the manuscript and audience without first supplying our diagnosis. That was a way to seek another reading; it was not equivalent to independent human or scientific review. A different model also brought suggestions, not a verdict. We judged those suggestions against passages and sources.

For future work, we would keep a concise decision record outside the conversation. It would distinguish the intended audience, accepted editorial decisions, verified source constraints, and unresolved questions. A fresh reviewer would initially receive only the manuscript, audience, and review task. Accepted findings would then return to the working record.

## A repeatable approach to try next

| Stage | What to produce | What would justify moving on |
|---|---|---|
| Define the book | A short reader brief and a distinct job for each chapter | The chapters build understanding, including objections and unresolved tensions. |
| Establish support | A record of consequential claims, sources, inspected material, and limits | The wording distinguishes evidence from interpretation and illustration. |
| Test the writing | Two substantial passages that require different treatments | Readers can follow the explanation and identify what kept them interested. |
| Draft and revise | A working manuscript plus brief reasons for consequential edits | Each edit addresses an identified problem and preserves the intended claim. |
| Read afresh | Passage-specific feedback collected before explaining our intentions | The reader's response can be compared with the actual text. |
| Integrate and release | A fixed manuscript, rebuilt editions, and a concise change record | Changed claims and production checks pass; remaining limitations are stated. |

We would avoid growing a large style system before testing actual writing, asking every chapter to use the same sequence, treating model agreement as verification, and repeatedly requesting a general improvement pass without naming a problem. A useful stopping rule is that a change must offer a clear benefit to understanding, interest, accuracy, or progression.

Human readers should enter earlier than they did here. Specialists can assess claims and interpretations that ordinary reading cannot settle. Neither kind of feedback has yet been collected systematically for this edition.
