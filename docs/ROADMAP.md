# Next steps

The complete beta and its supporting files are prepared. The next revision needs information from readers and reviewers.

1. **Get reader feedback.** Give the same copy to a small group of intended readers. Ask them to mark where they became interested, started skimming, or got confused. Collect their reactions before explaining what a passage was meant to do.
2. **Check the important claims.** Record each central claim, its source, what was inspected, and any limits the wording must keep. Prioritize causal and health claims, contested findings, and sources previously checked only through abstracts. Ask independent specialists to review the relevant passages.
3. **Revise against the findings.** Address factual problems, misunderstandings, and repeated points where readers lost interest. Record why each substantial change was made.
4. **Prepare the next reading copy.** Finish copyediting, rebuild the editions, and inspect their layout and navigation. Choose whether the book will remain short before adding material.

The GitHub project can share the current beta and process with these limits clearly stated. The manuscript and other project materials still need a decision about reuse licensing before general reuse is invited.
