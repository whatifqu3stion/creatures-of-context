# Editorial evaluation

Run this after editing. A hard-gate failure blocks delivery.

## Hard gates: truth and meaning

- [ ] No claim, source, statistic, quotation, example, opinion, scene detail, or motive was invented.
- [ ] The author's central point and consequential distinctions remain intact.
- [ ] Causal, correlational, speculative, and metaphorical language remain correctly separated.
- [ ] Uncertainty, disagreement, population limits, and scope limits that affect interpretation remain visible.
- [ ] Author synthesis is not presented as an established scientific finding.
- [ ] No transition implies support, chronology, or consensus absent from the supplied material.
- [ ] Humor, imagery, simplification, and narrative structure do not distort the subject.
- [ ] Any unresolved factual or interpretive ambiguity is flagged rather than silently repaired.

## Voice preservation

- [ ] The revision preserves recognizable vocabulary, cadence, emotional register, and level of formality.
- [ ] Strong distinctive sentences remain unless a concrete problem required change.
- [ ] The edit did not make every paragraph equally tidy or rhythmically similar.
- [ ] Useful digressions, admissions, uncertainty, and rough edges remain when they contribute character or thought.
- [ ] Voice consistency does not erase purposeful tonal variation between narrative, explanation, argument, and reflection.

## Clarity and accessibility

- [ ] Difficult ideas are opened up rather than reduced.
- [ ] Necessary technical terms are explained or contextualized, not replaced with misleading approximations.
- [ ] Sentences are intelligible on first attentive reading without becoming uniformly short.
- [ ] Concrete facts, mechanisms, and examples survive the edit.
- [ ] Abstraction, jargon, and nominalization remain only when they carry necessary meaning.

## Narrative and craft

- [ ] The opening creates a real reason to continue without generic throat clearing.
- [ ] Revelations are earned rather than announced.
- [ ] The prose avoids repeated templates, fake profundity, inflated importance, vague authority, and decorative formatting.
- [ ] Sentence and paragraph shapes respond to the movement of thought.
- [ ] The ending serves the chapter's argument or handoff rather than merely recapping or producing a slogan.
- [ ] Humor, if present, arises naturally and does not feel assigned.

## Final comparison

- [ ] A sentence-by-sentence comparison found no accidental loss of substantive content.
- [ ] The revision is no more extensive than the problems required.
- [ ] Read aloud, the passage sounds like an intelligent person communicating—not a system performing "human" style.
- [ ] The change note names material choices without overstating what the edit accomplished.

If a hard gate fails, fix the revision or return the original language with a question. If a softer craft check fails because the author made a deliberate choice, preserve the choice.
