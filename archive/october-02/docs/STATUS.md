# Current state

Book: 2 October 2026 beta. Documentation updated 6 October 2026.

A complete short-book draft is available: an introduction, fourteen chapters, a conclusion, and 63 source notes. It has about 22,100 words of main text and 101 PDF pages.

The PDF, EPUB, and Markdown come from the same chapter files. The final style pass has a preserved earlier draft, a record of replacements, and [three worked examples](../methods/WORKED_EXAMPLES.md). Those examples show the edits and their intended purpose; reader feedback has not yet established their effect.

## What has been checked

The [recorded technical checks](../records/TECHNICAL_QA.json) passed for source/output consistency, notes, internal navigation, and PDF text bounds. The [visual review](../records/VISUAL_QA.md) covered every page in contact sheets and selected pages at reading size. It caught an isolated final sentence, which was brought back onto the preceding page.

Scientific checking was less uniform. It used full papers, abstracts, official guidance, and earlier research records. The [evidence log](../records/EVIDENCE_CHANGES.md) describes the checks made during the final style pass. It is not a complete audit of the book.

## What is still needed

- Feedback from readers who have not followed this project.
- A claim-by-claim evidence review, with independent specialist input on central scientific and health interpretations.
- Final copyediting, print layout decisions, and checks in e-reader applications.

The GitHub package is prepared, but publication remains pending. No general reuse license has been chosen for the manuscript and project materials. The included editing skill keeps its own license.

The [next steps](ROADMAP.md) focus on collecting feedback and using it to revise specific passages.
