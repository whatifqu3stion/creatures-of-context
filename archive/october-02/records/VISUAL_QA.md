# Visual review of the reader beta

2 October 2026. Final PDF: 101 pages; SHA-256 is recorded in `EDITION_MANIFEST.json`.

All pages were rendered and inspected in six contact sheets. Pages 20, 27, 55, 65, 90, 91, and 101 were additionally inspected at reading size, covering an added experiment, the marshmallow passage, a later chapter opening, the final page, and source notes. The review checked hierarchy, readable type, margins, superscript notes, URL wrapping, and page flow.

The first build had an isolated concluding sentence on its own page. Body leading was adjusted from 15.4 to 15 points while retaining 10.5-point type. The regenerated conclusion ends with its preceding discussion. Some short chapter-ending pages remain; this is a beta reading layout rather than a finished print imposition.

No clipping, overlapping text, blank pages, or unresolved note markup was observed in the reviewed renders. Automated bounds and internal-link checks also passed. Rendering review does not verify scientific claims or guarantee accessibility in every reader. EPUB XML and destinations were checked; EPUB application-specific visual testing remains open.
